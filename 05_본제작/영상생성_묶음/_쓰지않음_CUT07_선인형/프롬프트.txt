===== 복사 =====
A still-life shot of an inanimate felt doll, filmed like a product photograph. The doll is an inanimate handmade object and stays frozen in every frame: no blinking, no breathing, no change of expression. The eyes, eyebrows, mouth, head, hair, hoodie and arms are identical in the first and last frame. Locked-off tripod camera: no push-in, no zoom, no pan. Focus and lighting stay constant. The only movement is a very faint shimmer in the blurred lights far behind the doll. Quiet night ambience only. No voice, no music, no subtitles.
