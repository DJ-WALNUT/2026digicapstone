===== 복사 =====
A still-life shot of an inanimate cloth doll, filmed like a product photograph. Every part of the doll is frozen for the entire shot: the bead eyes, embroidered mouth, head, yarn hair and hoodie are identical in the first and last frame. No blinking, no breathing, no change of expression. Locked-off tripod camera: no push-in, no zoom, no pan; the framing of the last frame is the same as the first frame. Focus and lighting stay constant. The only movement is faint dust drifting in the morning light. Near silence, one tiny thread creak. No voice, no music, no subtitles.
